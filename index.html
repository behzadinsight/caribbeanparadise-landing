FAQ

Common questions from Afghan applicants

Is this programme legal?

Yes. The Dominica passport is offered through the country's official Citizenship by Investment Programme, established by an act of parliament.

Is physical presence in Dominica required?

No. There is no residency requirement before, during, or after the application. The entire process can be completed without travelling to Dominica.

What if my application is rejected?

During the free initial assessment we identify any factors that could lead to rejection — before any payment is made. In rare rejection cases, we walk you through the official appeals process.

How does the payment process work?

Two options: payment after receiving the passport (in special cases) or payment in three stages aligned with each official milestone. Both are documented in your engagement agreement.

Can I apply with my family?

Yes. The main applicant can include spouse and children in the same application. In some cases, additional family members can also be added.

How long does it take?

For most Afghan applicants, the timeline is 4 to 8 months from file opening to passport issuance, subject to government review timelines.

How can I get started?

Begin with a free, no-obligation case assessment — by WhatsApp, by phone, or in person at one of our offices.
